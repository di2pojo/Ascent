How Ascent works

You live with your parents and you are going to build a superintelligence. Twelve model generations stand between your bedroom and Omega. Whether that ending is good depends on whether you can still steer what you built.

Getting around

Walking
WASD or the arrow keys, Shift to run. On a phone, use the stick in the corner or tap the ground to walk there. Tap a door to walk to it and go in.
Doing things
Walk up to a door, a counter, a person or a spot on your lab floor and a button appears. Press it, or E on a keyboard.
The map
Top right. The orange dot is you, the pulsing ring is where the tutorial wants you to go.

Around town

🖥️ Bits & Bolts
The hardware store. Buy machines, carry them to your lab and install them on a free floor spot. Cloud pods are rented, so they start working without being carried. You can sell back anything you are holding.
📚 Data Exchange
Training data subscriptions. Data piles up every second and each training run swallows a big chunk.
⚡ Kraftwerk Power
Your electricity bill, supply upgrades that raise your lab's power ceiling, and deals that cut the rate.
👥 Talent Agency
Hire engineers, scientists and alignment researchers. They show up at a desk in your lab.
🏦 Northgate Capital
Raise funding rounds in exchange for equity. Investors have a checklist: revenue, money earned so far, trust, team, products, audits and where you stand against the frontier.
🏠 Estate agent
Lease the next property once you've filled half of the current one and have the team and revenue to justify it. Movers take your machines and staff with you.
📣 Buzz PR
Launch products and run press campaigns.
🛡️ Safety Institute
Commission independent audits that raise alignment.

How a model gets made

Pretraining
The model reads your whole stockpile of tokens and learns to predict the next one. It eats almost all the compute, needs cash, data and research up front, and occasionally hits a loss spike that rolls it back to the last checkpoint. Engineers make spikes rarer.
The base model
What pretraining produces. It can continue text and nothing else, so it can't be shipped as is.
Post-training
Supervised fine-tuning teaches it to follow instructions. RLHF has annotators rank answers, trains a reward model on their preferences and optimises against it; that is where most of the safety comes from. Reasoning and safety RL goes further still. You pick a tier: cheaper and faster, or safer and more valuable.
Evaluate and ship
Red-teaming costs money and time and keeps alignment up. Shipping untested is quick and costs alignment.
Compute allocation
A shipped model needs compute to answer customers; the rest is split between the training run and research experiments, which earn research every second. Idle machines run experiments automatically.
Earning research
The Code app has short tasks. Ranking answers, labelling data and spotting duplicates need no coding; the coding ones pay a little more. Settings can hide the coding ones.
Research focus
A slider that splits research output. The alignment share keeps the model you shipped understood; the capability share builds the next one.
Oversight
Above 1.0x alignment climbs, below it falls. Every model you ship raises the bar.
Control margin
The gap between capability and alignment. The wider it is, the more often things go wrong, from outages to models acting outside what you authorised.

The competition

Rival labs
Three of them, and they start shipping once you do. The Market app has the leaderboard.
Catching up
The field learns from itself: rivals can only get so far ahead of your deployed model, and while you're behind, your research runs up to 50% faster from published work. You can be overtaken, but never left behind for good.
Obsolescence
Revenue depends on where your model stands. Leading pays well; a model that has been overtaken earns a fraction of what it did, until you ship something better.

Days

The clock
Shops open 08:00 to 20:00. Sleep in your bed from 18:00 to end the day; stay up past midnight and you pass out. Overnight, training keeps running, money moves and rivals announce their releases. You read it all in the morning report.

Money and power

Trust
Public trust settles toward a level set by your alignment, whether you lead the frontier, your products and your audits. Releases and press campaigns lift it for a while; incidents and being overtaken knock it down. Investors and product launches need it.
The burn
Salaries, rent, electricity and cloud fees come out every second. The small number under your cash is net flow. Red means trouble.
Power
Every machine draws kilowatts against your building's ceiling. Above 85% the supply starts tripping and interrupts training.
